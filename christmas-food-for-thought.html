Cranberry-Jalapeño Cream Cheese Dip

Ingredients

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup whole-berry cranberry sauce
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and finely minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Crackers or tortilla chips, to serve

Instructions

  1. Spread the softened cream cheese in an even layer on a small plate or shallow bowl.
  2. Stir the cranberry sauce, jalapeño, cilantro, and lime juice together, then spoon over the top.
  3. Serve with crackers or chips for scooping.

3-Ingredient Cranberry Shortbread Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup salted butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries, chopped

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 325°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Beat the butter and powdered sugar until smooth, then stir in the flour and cranberries to form a dough.
  3. Roll into 1-inch balls, flatten slightly, and space on the sheet.
  4. Bake 15 to 18 minutes, until the edges are just golden. Cool on the sheet.
