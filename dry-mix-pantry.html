Taco Seasoning

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup chili powder
  • 2 tbsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix it: Whisk everything together in a bowl, or shake it in the jar. Store airtight, away from the stove.
Chef's tips
  • Makes about 2/3 cup (5 packets).
  • 2 tablespoons of mix equals one store-bought packet.
  • A store packet holds about 2 tablespoons of seasoning. One small jar of this mix replaces five of them.
  • Storage: Keeps 6 months, store in a half-pint jar.
Ways to use it
  • Taco night: Brown 1 lb ground beef or turkey and drain. Stir in 2 tbsp mix and 2/3 cup water. Simmer 5 minutes, until thick.
  • Taco chicken: Toss 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs with 2 tbsp mix and 1 tbsp oil. Roast at 425°F for 25 minutes (165°F inside).
  • Fiesta dip: Stir 1 tbsp mix into 1 cup sour cream. Serve with chips and veggies.

Cowboy Cookies in a Jar

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour mixed with 1/2 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp cinnamon and 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/3 cup shredded coconut
  • 1/3 cup chopped pecans

Instructions

  1. Layer it: Fill a clean, dry 1-quart jar in the order listed, packing each layer firmly with a spoon.
  2. Beat 1/2 cup soft butter, 1 egg and 1 tsp vanilla in a large bowl.
  3. Add the jar contents and stir until combined.
  4. Bake 1 1/2 tbsp scoops on a lined sheet at 350°F for 10 to 12 minutes.
Chef's tips
  • Makes about 2 1/2 dozen cookies.
  • Pack each layer down firmly with a spoon so everything fits.
  • Did you know? Laura Bush's Texas Cowboy Cookies won a magazine presidential cookie bake-off in 2000.
  • Storage: Keeps 3 months, store in a 1-quart wide-mouth jar.
