Maple-Pecan Baked Camembert

Prep: 5 minutesCook: 15 minutesServes: 2–4

Ingredients

  • 1 small (8 oz) wheel Camembert
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup chopped pecans
  • 1 tsp fresh thyme leaves
  • Crackers or sliced baguette, to serve

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Place the Camembert in a small baking dish.
  2. Score the top rind lightly in a crosshatch pattern.
  3. Drizzle with maple syrup, then scatter pecans and thyme over the top.
  4. Bake 12–15 minutes until soft and molten in the center.
  5. Serve immediately with crackers or baguette for dipping.
Chef's tips
  • Short on pecans? Walnuts or sliced almonds work just as well.
  • Did you know? Camembert comes from Normandy in northern France. Local legend credits a farmer named Marie Harel with making the first one in the 1790s.

Turkey & Wild Rice Soup

Prep: 10 minutesCook: 35 minutesServes: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 6 cups turkey or chicken broth
  • 3/4 cup wild rice blend
  • 2 cups shredded leftover turkey
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Melt butter and sauté onion, carrots, and celery until softened, about 6 minutes.
  2. Add broth, wild rice, and thyme. Simmer 25 minutes until the rice is tender.
  3. Stir in turkey and cream; warm through for 5 minutes. Season to taste.
  4. Serve hot with crusty bread.
Chef's tips
  • This freezes well — make a double batch and stash some for a busy night.
  • Did you know? Wild rice isn't really rice. It's the seed of a tall water grass that grows in shallow lakes and rivers.
