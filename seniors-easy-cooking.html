Creamy Stovetop Oatmeal for Two

Prep: 2 minutesCook: 8 minutesServes: 2

Ingredients

  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup water
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 ripe banana, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Put the oats, milk, water and salt in a small saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle boil over medium heat, then turn the heat to low.
  3. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  4. Stir in half the banana and the cinnamon.
  5. Spoon into bowls and top with the rest of the banana and the syrup.
Chef's tips
  • For one, use half of everything. Leftover oatmeal keeps 3 days in the fridge. Reheat with a splash of milk.
  • Did you know? Oats are a whole grain. Old-fashioned rolled oats are oat kernels that have been steamed and flattened, so they cook quickly.
  • Good to know: Soft texture, one pot.

Mini Meatloaf for Two

Prep: 10 minutesCook: 35 minutesServes: 2

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb lean ground beef
  • 1/4 cup plain breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 tsp salt and a pinch of pepper
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Line a small baking pan with foil.
  2. Gently mix the beef, breadcrumbs, egg, milk, onion, salt and pepper.
  3. Shape into a small loaf about 6 inches long on the pan.
  4. Stir the ketchup and brown sugar together and spread on top.
  5. Bake 35 to 40 minutes, until the center reaches 160°F. Rest 5 minutes.
Chef's tips
  • Mix with a light hand. Squeezing the meat makes a tough meatloaf.
  • Good to know: Freezer-friendly.
